Who We Are

Transforming Vehicle Maintenance Through Mechanical Innovation

Born out of a drive to solve the persistent inefficiencies of conventional wash bays—excessive water usage, high labor costs, inconsistent wash quality, and scratch risks—we design systems that deliver showroom-grade finishes while drastically reducing operating overhead.

We are an automated vehicle-care innovation company blending pro-grade industrial hardware, Japanese-engineered control automation, eco-conscious chemistry, and hands-on technical support. Through our flagship Aqua Dragon platform, we deliver turnkey washing systems to motorcycle service centers, commercial wash hubs, automotive dealerships, and independent business operators looking for scalable, high-margin wash bay operations.

🎯

Our Mission

To design, manufacture, and deliver high-quality automated vehicle-washing systems that combine innovative engineering, intelligent automation, water and energy efficiency, and reliable performance—helping businesses achieve greater productivity, profitability, and customer satisfaction while contributing to a cleaner and more sustainable future.

🔭

Our Vision

To become a globally recognized innovator in intelligent vehicle-washing technology, creating cleaner, smarter, and more sustainable washing solutions that transform the way vehicles are maintained.

Engineering Foundation

Our 4 Core Pillars

Every machine manufactured at our Bulupitiya facility adheres to rigorous engineering standards.

🛡️

Pro-Grade Industrial Build

Built to last in harsh bay environments using heavy-duty SS 304 austenitic stainless steel frames and hot-dip galvanized anti-corrosion structural steel.

🤖

Intelligent Automation

Powered by advanced industrial PLC automation and proprietary thrust-vector nozzle motion for complete vehicle contour coverage without human error.

💧

Eco-Efficient Engineering

Precision chemical dosing and optimized spray geometries engineered to cut water and electrical consumption by a significant margin compared to continuous manual hose operation.

✨

Scratch-Safe, Contactless Care

Automated, high-impact dynamic sprays and snow-foam encapsulation protect vehicle paint from abrasive swirl marks caused by contact washing.
