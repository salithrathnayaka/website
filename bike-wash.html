Cycle Breakdown (One-Touch 4s Activation)

Automated 3-Stage Cleaning Sequence

Simply ride the motorcycle into the bay, press the start button, and let the robotics complete the full cycle in under 8 minutes.

Stage 01 • 30 Seconds

Degreaser Spray

Specialized chemical dosing applies targeted solvent to break down chain lube, exhaust grease, and stubborn road pitch on engine fins and swingarm.

Stage 02 • 30 Seconds

Snow Foam Shampoo

Thick clinging active foam encapsulates dirt particles, lifting abrasive road sand away from delicate fairings, headlights, and polished paintwork.

Stage 03 • 3 Min 15 Sec

High-Pressure Wash

Dynamic oscillating dual robotic nozzles deliver high-velocity water jets across 360-degree vectors, flushing away foam and grime for a sparkling showroom finish.
