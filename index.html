About Riotive

Pioneering Intelligent Autowash Engineering

At Riotive Autowash Innovations (Pvt) Ltd, we engineer intelligent, automated vehicle washing solutions that replace slow, manual labor with high-throughput mechanical precision.

Born out of a drive to solve the inefficiencies of conventional wash bays—excessive water usage, high labor costs, inconsistent wash quality, and scratch risks—we design systems that deliver showroom-grade finishes while drastically reducing operating overhead.

End-to-End Operational Partnership

We do not just manufacture hardware; we provide complete, end-to-end technical support across every phase of your business:

  • ✓ On-Site Installation & Assembly: Tailored mechanical setup to your bay layout.
  • ✓ Pressure Commissioning: Strict calibration of spray vectors, sensors, and water pressure.
  • ✓ Hands-On Operator Training: Certified training for your crew to ensure safe, smooth operation.
  • ✓ 24/7 Breakdown Dispatch & Warranty: Direct component replacement & zero-downtime policy.

Pro-Grade Industrial Build

Intelligent Automation
